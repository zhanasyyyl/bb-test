Get Ready for the Digital SAT

Get Ready for the Digital SAT

If you’re taking the test on a weekend, here’s how it’ll work.

Before test day, head to the Practice and Prepare section of the Bluebook homepage and start practicing.

You can explore the tools and features of the app and try a few sample questions in the test preview or take a full-length practice test.

The week of the test, you'll complete a quick exam setup to check your device and get your admission ticket.

You’ll need this admission ticket on test day. You can take a picture of it, print it, or email it to yourself.

Arrive on time on test day. Check your admission ticket for your arrival time and the address of your test center.

Be sure to bring your fully charged device. It’ll need to stay on for roughly three hours, so we recommend you bring a power cord or portable charger. You’ll also need your admission ticket, a valid photo ID, and a pencil or pen. Scratch paper will be provided.

The digital SAT has two sections—Reading and Writing, and Math. It should take you just over 2 hours to complete, not including breaks.

Each section of the test has two parts called modules, and each module is timed separately. You can move back and forth between questions in a module and review your answers until time expires. There will be a break between sections.

During the test, you’ll have access to a set of tools.

On all math questions, you’ll find a reference sheet and a calculator. You can also bring an approved calculator.

Click Highlights & Notes, then select some text to highlight, underline, or leave yourself a note.

On multiple-choice questions, if you think an answer option is wrong, you can cross it out.

You can use the line reader to help you focus as you’re reading test content.

And you can mark for review any questions you want to come back to later.

Zoom in and out using keyboard shortcuts on laptops or by pinching on tablets.

If you have a problem during the test, click the Help icon for troubleshooting tips. And you can always raise your hand to ask the proctor for help, too.

When the test is over, your answers will be submitted automatically. Wait until you see the “Congratulations” screen to close your device.

We’re excited for you to take the digital SAT. Good luck on test day!

{# ===== Test Day Checklist ===== #}

Test Day Checklist

REQUIRED:

  • You must bring this fully charged device—it needs to stay on for roughly 3 hours.
  • Bring your admission ticket, which you can view in Bluebook. You can print it or email it to yourself.
  • You’ll need a valid photo ID on test day.
  • Follow all safety procedures at your school or test center.

ALLOWED:

  • You should bring a power cord or a portable charger, but we can’t guarantee you’ll have access to an outlet.
  • There’s a calculator built into the app, but you can also bring your own approved calculator.
  • You can bring an external mouse or keyboard. You can use an external keyboard with a tablet—not with a laptop.
  • You’re allowed snacks and water, but only during breaks.

PROHIBITED:

  • Smart glasses, smartphones, and smartwatches are prohibited. If you bring one, the proctor will collect it before the test.
  • Other applications—you must close all other apps and programs while the test is in progress.
  • Notes, books, or any other reference materials.
  • You will be provided scratch paper, but you may not bring your own.
  • Headphones or earbuds—unless you have a College Board–approved accommodation.
  • Any camera, recording device, or timer.
{# ===== Don't See Your Test Here? ===== #}

Don't See Your Test Here?

If You're Taking an AP Exam

If you're registered for an AP Exam, you'll see your exam listed on the Bluebook app homepage five days before your test date. Make sure you’re signed in to Bluebook with a College Board account.

If it's within five days of your exam, and it's not showing up in the app, please contact your teacher or your AP coordinator.

If You’re Taking a PSAT/NMSQT, PSAT 10, or PSAT 8/9

If you're registered for any PSAT-related assessment, your school will give you a sign-in ticket that you’ll use to access Bluebook. Then you'll see your test listed on the homepage.

If you signed in with a College Board account, you need to sign out and then sign back in using your sign-in ticket.

If your test is still not showing up, please contact your teacher or someone at your school.

If You’re Taking an SAT

If you're registered to take the SAT on a weekend, you'll see it listed on the Bluebook homepage five days before your test date. Make sure you’re signed in to Bluebook with a College Board account (if not, you need to sign out then sign back in).

If you're taking the SAT on a school day, your school will give you a sign-in ticket that you’ll use to access Bluebook (if you signed in with a College Board account, you need to sign out and then sign back in).

If it's within five days of your test, and it's not showing up in the app, please contact your teacher or your SAT coordinator.

{# ===== Bluebook Practice ===== #}

Bluebook Practice

AP Exam Previews

These short previews will give you a way to see real AP questions in the app, try out the testing tools, and practice testing with any assistive technology you may use.

AP Exam previews will not be scored.

Full-Length AP Practice

Full-length AP practice is not available in Bluebook. Your AP teacher can assign practice exams to you in AP Classroom. Please check there to see what practice resources are available for your course.

Full-Length PSAT/NMSQT, PSAT 10, or PSAT 8/9 Practice

If you take a full-length practice test for any of the PSAT-related assessments, your scores will be available on My Practice. Make sure you sign in to My Practice the same way you signed in to Bluebook to see your practice test scores.

If you take a practice test more than once, you'll just see one listing for it on the Past tab under Practice and Prepare. But when you go to My Practice, each completed test will be listed.

SAT Test Preview

This short preview gives you a way to see real SAT questions in the app, try out the testing tools, and practice testing with any assistive technology you may use.

If you take the SAT test preview, you won't get a score. To get a score on a practice test, return to the homepage and select SAT Full-Length Practice.

Full-Length SAT Practice

If you take a full-length SAT practice test, your scores will be available on My Practice. Make sure you sign in to My Practice the same way you signed in to Bluebook to see your practice test scores.

If you take a practice test more than once, you'll just see one listing for it on the Past tab under Practice and Prepare. But when you go to My SAT, each completed test will be listed.
